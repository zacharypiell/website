About Me

I've always lived at the intersection of structure and creativity. By day, I'm a Chicago-based technology consultant, currently completing my One-Year MBA at Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management.

Off the clock, that same energy shifts to sound and visual art. I've played piano and guitar for over a decade, producing and releasing 20+ tracks along the way. Behind the lens, I focus on drone photography, chasing clean geometry, aerial perspectives, and dramatic landscapes.

Whether I'm architecting a system, building a melody, or framing a shot from four hundred feet up, the drive is identical: creating something compelling from the ground up.
